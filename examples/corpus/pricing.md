# Plans and Pricing

Beacon is free and open source under the Apache 2.0 license. Quillstack sells hosted plans and support for organisations that prefer not to run it themselves.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Seats | Monthly active users |
|------|-------|-------|----------------------|
| Community | Free | Unlimited | Unlimited (self-hosted) |
| Team | $25 per seat per month | Up to 50 | 100,000 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited |

The Team plan includes SSO, experiment dashboards and email support with a two-business-day response time. The Enterprise plan adds a 99.95% uptime SLA, audit log export, custom data residency in the EU or US, and a named support engineer with a four-hour response time for severity-one incidents.

## Billing

Hosted plans are billed monthly by default. Annual billing gives a 20% discount. Monthly active users (MAU) are counted as unique user keys evaluated in a calendar month; if a Team workspace exceeds 100,000 MAU for two consecutive months, Quillstack will contact you about upgrading but will not cut off evaluations.

## Free trial

Every new hosted workspace starts with a 14-day free trial of the Team plan. No credit card is required, and the workspace falls back to read-only mode if you do not choose a plan when the trial ends.
